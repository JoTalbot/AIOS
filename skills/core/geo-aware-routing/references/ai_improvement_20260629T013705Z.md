### Назначение

**Название:** Geo-Aware Routing (Гео-свободный маршрутизатор)

**Описание:**
Optimizes swarm traffic based on geographic location and latency. Maps nodes to regions (eu-west, eu-central, us-east, asia-east). Builds routing tables sorted by latency and cost.
